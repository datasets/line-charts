The [front page](https://linecharts.datahub.io/) now puts the seven libraries side by side: stars and downloads, size, how fast each draws 1.25 million points, how much each did for us, and how much code it took. A chart shows the trade-off behind the picks, and a new section shows each library out of the box next to the version we styled, because styled to one brief they all look alike. "Try it" now opens on all 17 years, with each library's render time on its button. A switcher shows every chart we built, the same chart across all seven, in ranking order, and a [Why page](https://linecharts.datahub.io/why) says why we did this, with examples from DataHub.

How the front page changed in the run-up to launch:

- **September:** seven copies of one synthetic chart, each with its line count and render time.

- **21 November, morning:** a hero that says what we did, beside the oil chart, with picks by job after the scroll.

![A hero beside the oil chart](assets/changelog/2026-11-21-evolution-hero.png)

- **21 November, evening:** the side-by-side table, the trade-off chart, and the libraries out of the box.
