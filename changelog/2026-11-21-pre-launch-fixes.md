title: Pre-launch fixes from an independent review
promote: false
---

An independent review before launch found two broken controls and some claims that went further than the evidence, all now fixed. Plotly's Indexed and Small multiples views drew blank, and µPlot could not pan when embedded on the front page; both work now, on touch too. Observable Plot and Vega-Lite now draw the dense grid chart as a daily mean over a daily low-to-high band, so they show the true solar peak instead of flattening it, and the ranking now states the criteria it weighs. The grid's rule marks the closure of Ratcliffe, Britain's last coal plant, with the later stray coal readings stated rather than hidden; the 2012 coal share can be checked against a committed file; and the missing-data test now uses World Bank life expectancy with the removed years listed.
